Our Roots

Born in Unjha — India's spice city.

Unjha in North Gujarat is home to Asia's largest seed spice market. The cumin and fennel traded here set benchmark prices for the whole country — and the region's farms produce some of the finest jeera in the world.

Keyur Spices was born in this tradition. We source the best lots right at the source, process them with care, and pack them under our own two brands — Gold for premium buyers and Silver for everyday value.
